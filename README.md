# deliverydatesresearch.com
Delivery Dates Research — https://deliverydatesresearch.com

Delivery Dates Research compares the delivery dates data-center operators promise with what their later SEC filings report. Promises are cheap. Filings are DD.

## Current figures

29 of 79 past-due delivery promises slipped (36.7%): 21 delivered late, 8 moved later. Among those delivered late, the median was 62 days late, range 1 to 366.
