Delivery Dates Research

I read SEC filings for the delivery dates data-center operators promise, then match each promise to what a later filing reports. Promises are cheap. Filings are DD.

Current coverage is 8 U.S.-listed companies and 79 past-due tracked promises. Of those 79, 29 slipped (36.7%) — 21 delivered late and 8 moved to a later date. Among the late deliveries, the median was 62 days late, with a range of 1 to 366 days.

I hold no position in any of the 8 covered companies.
