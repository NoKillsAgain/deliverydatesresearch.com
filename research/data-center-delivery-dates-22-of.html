Data-center delivery dates: 29 of 79 past-due promises slipped

Originally published Oct 26, 2026 on Substack.

Delivery Dates Research · October 26, 2026

Corrected October 26, 2026: a re-check against later SEC filings moved five promises to slipped and three to delivered on time. The figures below are updated. What changed and why: deliverydatesresearch.com/corrections.html

Updated November 3, 2026: one TeraWulf promise was reclassified from delivered late to moved to a later date, after its February 7, 2023 8-K gave only an expected quarter. The slipped total is unchanged at 29 of 79 (36.7%). Late deliveries came a median of 62 days late, range 1 to 366.

Free post. Findings compare each company’s own filed statements with what it filed later. They are opinions based on public records, not claims of wrongdoing.

This note compares the dated delivery promises that 8 U.S.-listed data-center operators made in their own SEC filings with the same companies’ subsequent filings.

  • 79 promises are past the first date the company gave.

  • 41 were delivered on time, 21 delivered late, 8 moved to a later date, 2 delivered with no filing saying when, and 7 have no later word.

  • 29 of 79 (36.7%) slipped: delivered late, moved to a later date, or dropped.

  • When delivery came late, it came a median of 62 days after the first date given. The range was 1 to 366 days.

A vague promised date is due on the last day its wording allows ("Q2" means June 30). Vague proof counts from the first day of its period.

Companies covered: TeraWulf, Soluna, Bitdeer, IREN, Applied Digital, HIVE, Cipher and Keel (formerly Bitfarms). Several companies have only a few promises in the count; company-level rates should be read with that in mind.
