The CB-4 dates, filing by filing

Originally published Nov 15, 2026 on Substack.

TeraWulf’s Lake Mariner building CB-4 has been dated three ways in its SEC filings: a third-quarter 2026 lease start, then delivery in the second half of 2026, then the fourth quarter.

TeraWulf has now given three calendars for CB-4 at Lake Mariner. Here they are, in filing order, in the company’s own words.

October 2025: a third-quarter lease start

TeraWulf’s October 2025 investor presentation assumed CB-4 lease commencement in 3Q26. The line sits under “Key Model Assumptions” in Exhibit 99.1 of the Oct. 6, 2025 8-K:

“1. Full CB-2 lease commencement in 4Q25, CB-3 lease commencement in 1Q26, CB-4 lease commencement in 3Q26, CB-5 lease commencement in 4Q26”

That is a model assumption in an investor presentation, not a line from an earnings release.

February 2026: energization expected in the third quarter

From Exhibit 99.1 of the Feb. 26, 2026 8-K, under building-level construction status:

“CB4 (168 MW) – Energization expected Q3 2026”

The filing writes the building as “CB4,” without the hyphen used in later releases. We have not found a later filing, including the Nov. 6, 2026 8-K and the same day’s 10-Q (acc 0001628280-26-071671), that says CB-4 was energized. The phrase “has entered commissioning” in the August release below is not a statement that the building was energized.

August 2026: delivery in the second half

From Exhibit 99.1 of the Aug. 5, 2026 8-K:

“The first CB-4 data hall has entered commissioning, with phased delivery and rent commencement expected during the second half of 2026, while CB-5 remains targeted to begin phased delivery in early 2027.”

The lease-start window that had been the third quarter is now the second half of 2026. That is a move later. We are not treating it as a late delivery. A move is information, not wrongdoing.

November 2026: the fourth quarter

From Exhibit 99.1 of the Nov. 6, 2026 8-K:

“CB-4 remains on track for phased delivery and rent commencement during the fourth quarter.”

That sentence does not name the year. The same 8-K’s Exhibit 99.2 does: “CB-4: 168 MW phased Nov–Dec 2026” and “CB-4 (168 MW): phased Q4 delivery under amended lease schedule”.

The fourth quarter, and November to December, sit inside the second half already named in August. We are not treating the November 8-K as a further move. It narrows the remaining window. The filings also say “phased” and, in August, “The first CB-4 data hall,” so the window is for delivery in phases, not necessarily the last hall. The November 8-K does not say CB-4 has been delivered, or energized.

The same day’s 10-Q

TeraWulf’s Q3 2026 10-Q (acc 0001628280-26-071671), accepted Nov. 6, 2026, at 1:07 PM PT, after the 8-K that morning, does not name CB-4 as delivered or energized. CB-4 appears only as a lease commencement date term: “the CB-4 or CB-5 commencement dates under the Fluidstack HPC Leases”.

On the Fluidstack HPC Leases, the 10-Q says: “As of September 30, 2026, one of the Fluidstack HPC Leases had commenced and the remaining leases are expected to commence in the fourth quarter of 2026 and in early 2027.”

The same filing also says: “The amendments also provide for commencement on a data hall-by-data hall basis, with the affected leases expected to commence in phases during the fourth quarter of 2026 and early 2027.”

A separate sentence, on the Akela Fluidstack Leases, says: “As of September 30, 2026, one Akela Fluidstack Lease had commenced operations, with the remaining contracted capacity scheduled for phased commencement during the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first half of 2027.”

As of September 30, 2026, “the Lake Mariner Data Campus had 102 MW of energized critical IT HPC capacity and 145 MW of legacy bitcoin mining capacity”. A further, separate line in the same 10-Q says: “The campus is undergoing phased expansion to support additional contracted HPC deployments, with approximately 500 MW of aggregate gross capacity expected to be energized by early 2027, and planned expansion to approximately 750 MW, supported by an approved NYISO System Impact Study.”

The 10-Q does not say CB-4 was delivered, or energized. It does not say which halls are in the early-2027 group.

What we will check

The November 8-K still points at phased delivery and rent commencement in the fourth quarter of 2026. We have now read the same day’s 10-Q. We will read TeraWulf’s later filings, and we will re-check when a CB-4 commencement filing or 8-K appears, to see whether they say CB-4 was delivered in that window, and whether any filing says it was energized.

Dates of this kind do not always hold. Of the 79 past-due promises in our ledger, 29 slipped (36.7%): 21 were delivered late and 8 were moved later. Among those delivered late, the median was 62 days late, range 1 to 366. We are not changing that count. The dataset stays at 29 of 79 slipped.
