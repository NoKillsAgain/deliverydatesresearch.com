29 of 79 past-due delivery promises slipped

Originally published Nov 3, 2026 on Substack.

The current count from the companies’ own SEC filings. (A day late, because of technical difficulties.)

A quick note first: technical difficulties made this one 1 day late. That still beats the median we track.

We are currently tracking 79 past-due delivery promises that data-center companies made in their own SEC filings.

29 of 79 slipped (36.7%). That is 21 delivered late plus 8 moved later.

Among the late ones, the median was 62 days late, with a range of 1 to 366 days.

How we count: a promised date counts only when a later filing shows the thing promised actually happened by then. Starting isn’t finishing, and the last day of a reporting month gets no automatic credit. The full rules are on the website.
