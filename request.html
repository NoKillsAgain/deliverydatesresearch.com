How it works

From request to published report

1. Request. Send the ticker, your name or entity, and your relationship (if any) to the company. No payment yet.

2. Screening. We check you against our screening rules within 2 business days. If you pass, we send a payment link. If not, we decline and nothing is disclosed.

3. Research. We collect the company's dated delivery promises and compare them with its 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K filings. You get no preview and have no input.

4. Publication. The report is published with a disclosure label naming you and the amount paid. You get it at the same moment as everyone else, plus the source spreadsheet.

Screening rules

Who we decline

The company itself, its officers, directors, employees, affiliates, or anyone acting for it (including IR firms, PR agencies, law firms and placement agents).

Paid promoters, stock-promotion newsletters, and anyone compensated to promote or disparage the company or its securities.

Requesters who will not be named publicly, or who ask for a preview, an embargo they control, or any input on findings.

Requests tied to an announced deal, offering or proxy fight where the requester is a party. Declined requests are not disclosed.

Investors, analysts, funds, journalists and researchers are welcome, including those who hold long or short positions. Your position does not change the findings; we may ask you to state it, and we publish what you tell us.
