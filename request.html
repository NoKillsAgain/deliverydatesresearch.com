How it works

From request to published report

1. Request. Send the ticker, your name or entity, and your relationship (if any) to the company. No payment yet.

2. Screening. We check you against our screening rules within 2 business days. If you pass, we send a payment link. If not, we decline and nothing is disclosed.

3. Research. We collect the company's dated delivery promises and compare them with its 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K filings. You get no preview and have no input.

4. Publication. The report is published with a disclosure label naming you and the amount paid. You get it at the same moment as everyone else; the source spreadsheet is published with it for all readers.

Not investment advice

What this is, and what it is not

Analysis of public filings, not investment advice. Reports compare a company's public statements with its SEC filings. They do not say whether to buy, sell or hold any security, and they set no price targets or ratings.

No personalized recommendations. We don't know your situation, goals or holdings, and nothing we publish is tailored to you. Talk to a licensed adviser before you make investment decisions.

Same report for everyone. A requested report is the same report every reader gets. It is published to our general newsletter list and website on our regular publishing schedule, not sent privately first or written for one buyer.

No one-on-one advice. We don't take calls or answer questions about whether a requester or reader should trade a security. Questions about a report are limited to its method, sources and corrections. Please don't contact us for personalized investment advice.

As of the publication date. Each report reflects public filings and our analysis as of its publication date. It may be overtaken by later filings or events, and we have no duty to update it except through our corrections policy or a paid, labeled Update.

Screening rules

Who we decline

The company itself, its officers, directors, employees, affiliates, or anyone acting for it (including IR firms, PR agencies, law firms and placement agents).

Paid promoters, stock-promotion newsletters, and anyone compensated to promote or disparage the company or its securities.

Requesters who will not be named publicly, or who ask for a preview, an embargo they control, or any input on findings. Requesters cannot edit, approve, delay or veto findings, before or after publication.

Requesters who will not state in writing whether they hold, or plan within 3 days of publication to open or close, a position in the company. We publish that statement with the label.

Requests tied to an announced deal, offering or proxy fight where the requester is a party. Declined requests are not disclosed.

Investors, analysts, funds, journalists and researchers are welcome, including those who hold long or short positions. Your position does not change the findings; we may ask you to state it, and we publish what you tell us.

Trading policy

Our own trading

No trading by Delivery Dates Research or the people who prepare its reports in a covered company's securities from the earlier of request acceptance or 30 days before a release, through the later of 30 days after publication or 10 trading days after the release. This covers their immediate family and any account they control, and includes options and other derivatives.

If anyone preparing a report already holds the company's securities, they sell before work begins or step off that report.

We do not share findings with anyone, including the requester, before publication.

Corrections and terms

Corrections, liability and terms

Corrections. Anyone, including the company, can report an error with a source. Verified errors are corrected on the report within 2 business days, with a dated correction note; findings are never changed at a requester's request.

Liability. Reports are provided "as is" for information only. To the extent the law allows, Delivery Dates Research is not liable for losses from use of a report, and total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.

Terms. Requests are subject to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy; the Terms cover governing law and disputes. If you quote a report, quote it exactly and link to it.
